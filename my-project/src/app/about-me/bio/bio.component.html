Hello, I'm Hassan Gamal

I'm Hassan Gamal, a UX/UI & Product Designer who turns complex problems into simple, usable products. My path began in visual design — as a Graphic Designer at Pins-Egypt (2017) and Appliance Furniture (2019–2021) — before moving into digital as a Digital Specialist at Known-Group (2021), improving websites and user experiences.

In 2023, I completed an intensive UX/UI program at ITI, sharpening my skills in user research, design systems, and interaction design. Since 2024, I've been a UX/UI Designer at BI Technology, designing enterprise dashboards and data-driven applications end to end — from research and wireframes to high-fidelity UI — backed by frontend skills (Vue.js, Angular) that let me collaborate closely with engineers and ship what I design.
